# K1/X60 research scan and next measurements

Date: 2026-09-24. Target: MUSE-Pi-Pro, SpaceMiT K1/X60, Bianbu 2.3.5, 16 GB. This is a research and experiment plan, with one new targeted board measurement linked below. The board's `~/Projects` contains `llm-bench`, `spacemit-llama`, `spacemit-llama-frspec`, and `ccode`. The measured official fork is at `~/Projects/spacemit-llama/llama.cpp`, currently on `codex/lowacc-fallback` (`f3e71c9`); the mapped-head prototype is a separate checkout with a local modification to `src/models/qwen35.cpp`.

The [integrated K1 follow-up](2026-09-24-integrated-k1.md) builds and measures the ranked kernel and vocabulary candidates, tests a timing gate and hybrid dispatch, and records why the long-context routes were not ported for the current 8k workload.

## Current measured position

The [project baseline](2026-09-21-final.md) gives Qwen3.5-2B Q4_0 direct decode 5.19 tok/s and eight-stream aggregate 13.85 tok/s with the checkpoint/gate server; single-stream MTP with RS snapshot reached 6.31 tok/s. The [n-gram experiment](2026-09-24-ngram.md) reached 10.36 tok/s on a near-copy C++ edit, versus 7.99 for MTP alone, with nearly tied results on a small Python edit and prose prompt. The [mapped 32k head](2026-09-23-frspec.md) improved a tested Chinese prompt from 3.58 to 4.92 tok/s over the prefix 64k head, but direct decode was about 5.10 tok/s. These figures have different workloads and settings; they are not interchangeable.

The base 2B decode is already near the locally measured weight-stream bandwidth limit. The largest remaining opportunities are reducing *draft cost per accepted token*, improving the M=4–8 verification/multistream matrix path, and avoiding speculation when its measured request-level cost exceeds direct decode. Do not extrapolate a kernel-only speedup to end-to-end inference.

A new [same-model A/B](2026-09-24-mapped-cpu-sampling.md) combines the mapped 32k head with CPU-side MTP draft sampling: English reached **7.004 tok/s** versus 6.812 with backend sampling (+2.8%) and 5.118 direct; Chinese reached 5.075 versus 4.938 (+2.8%) but stayed below 5.115 direct. These are two-run, two-prompt server decode measurements in the experimental mapped-head checkout.

## Lower priority for today's 8k-context workload

- [Windowed-MTP](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.21535) bounds the draft KV read at very long contexts. Its reported gains are at roughly million-token context; first measure draft KV traffic at 8k/16k before changing attention.
- [KVBuffer](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.19049) and [TreeWY](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20961) reduce recurrent-state traffic or speculative state snapshots. The latter targets large hybrid models and memory-limited tree verification; this project has already implemented checkpoint rollback and found synchronization costly, so a direct port is not a near-term win.
- [upstream speculative modes](https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/blob/master/docs/speculative.md) and [Qwen3.5 compact-head issue #29145](https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/issues/29145) are useful integration references. The local prototype already implements a `d2t` mapping; upstream's open issue is not proof of a board speedup.

## Measurement gate

Use the existing [benchmark runner](../bench/README.md), the same official-fork base/model, `LD_LIBRARY_PATH` containing `spine-tcm`, fixed governor and affinity, alternating A/B order, at least three runs per arm, and identical greedy output hashes. Report prefill, decode, acceptance, and latency separately. Include English, Chinese, code, near-copy editing, long context, and concurrency 1/4/8. The mapped-head CPU-sampling result above is an encouraging targeted measurement; broader end-to-end improvement is still unestablished.
